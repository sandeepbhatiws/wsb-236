Our story

From a small curated shelf to a store people trust.

Lumora began in 2019 with a simple frustration: online shopping had become noisy. Thousands of near-identical listings, confusing prices and reviews you couldn't trust made buying something simple feel like work.

So we did the opposite. We test products ourselves, work directly with brands we believe in, and keep our catalogue intentionally small. Every item earns its place by being well made, fairly priced and genuinely useful.

Today we ship everyday essentials — from headphones and backpacks to planters and skincare — to customers across India, with the same promise we started with: shopping made simple.

Shipping policy

Orders ship within 1 business day. Standard delivery (4–6 business days) is free over ₹999, otherwise ₹79. Express (2–3 days, ₹149) and next-day (₹249, select cities) are available at checkout.

Returns & refunds

Return unused items in original packaging within 30 days. We arrange a free pickup, and refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of inspection.

Privacy policy

This demo stores your cart, wishlist and preferences only in your own browser (localStorage). No personal data is sent to a server, and no payment details are ever requested.
