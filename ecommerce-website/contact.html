FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 4–6 business days and is free on orders over ₹999. Express (2–3 days) and next-day delivery in select cities are available at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email and SMS with a tracking link. In this demo store, order tracking isn't connected to a real courier.

What is your return policy?

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days. We'll schedule a free pickup and refund you within 5–7 business days of inspection. See our returns policy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, UPI and major credit/debit cards. In this demo, UPI and card options are labelled as demonstrations and no payment is processed.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes — contact us within 2 hours of placing your order and we'll update or cancel it before it's dispatched.

Do you have coupon codes?

Try SAVE10 for 10% off (up to ₹1,000), WELCOME200 for ₹200 off orders above ₹1,499, or FREESHIP for free delivery. Apply them on the cart page.
